🎯 FEATURE IMPORTANCE - WHAT DRIVES BINDING
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

GRADIENT BOOSTING (RECOMMENDED):
  1. Molecular Weight (21.1%) - Size matters significantly
  2. H_total (20.9%) - Total H-bonding groups (donors + acceptors)
  3. H_Donors (20.2%) - Hydrogen bond donor count
  4. H_Acceptors (18.4%) - Hydrogen bond acceptor count
  5. MolWt_TPSA (8.95%) - Size-polarity interaction

KEY INSIGHT: H-BONDING DOMINANCE
  • Top 4 features (H-related + MolWt) account for 80.5% of importance
  • H-bonding is THE primary driver of binding affinity
  • Both molecular size AND polarity matter


RANDOM FOREST:
  1. H_total (20.3%) - Total H-bonding capacity
  2. MolWt_TPSA (16.3%) - Size-polarity product
  3. TPSA (15.3%) - Polar surface area
  4. H_Acceptors (14.6%) - H-acceptor count
  5. MolWt (10.8%) - Molecular weight

→ Agreement: Both models emphasize H-bonding and molecular properties
